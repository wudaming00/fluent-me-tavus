HOW TO READ THIS CHART It describes your voice signal, not your English level.
Waveform
Your relative microphone signal in this turn. Taller means stronger than other moments in the same turn.
Pitch line
The orange line estimates your voice moving higher or lower within this turn.
Pitch gaps
A gap means no confident periodic pitch was found. It is not an error and does not necessarily mean silence.
Blue pause band
A shaded band marks an estimated internal pause of at least 280 ms. Quiet speech, breath, or noise can affect it.

Read one turn at a time. Each chart is scaled independently, so do not compare waveform height or pitch position across turns. Microphone gain and distance also change the waveform.

What it cannot tell: this chart does not score pronunciation, accent, fluency, correct intonation, or emotion.
